# AgriPilot BD

## Platform

Web, responsive desktop and mobile.

## Users and purpose

Bangladeshi farmers exploring crop checks, field monitoring, climate information, yield scenarios, and general farming information in English and Bangla.

## Confirmed brief

Phase 1 is a demonstrative frontend. No real diagnosis, model accuracy, live weather, satellite feed, or validated yield prediction. No paid AI services. Local persistence and local advisor responses. Future self-hosted FastAPI connection through typed services.

## Brand commitments

Nature × Future. Forest #103C30, lime #C5F279, ivory #F7F8F2, leaf #397D5C, amber #E6A14B. Plus Jakarta Sans and Hind Siliguri. Real Bangladesh farming photography. Spacious app, clear controls, 18px cards and restrained motion.

## Implementation scope

Complete the retrieved Lovable foundation as a local Vite app. Seven primary screens, local alerts and profile/settings, accessible mobile navigation. All claims visibly illustrative. Hosted Lovable remains separate.
